Graphite’s “outer loop” framing: as AI speeds writing, review/test/merge/deploy become the bottleneck. Diamond (later folded into Graphite Agent) ships as a standalone AI reviewer—custom rules, contextual feedback, one-click suggested fixes—usable without buying the whole Graphite suite, free up to a monthly PR cap.

The strategic claim is toolchain completeness: AI review plus stacked PRs, merge queue, CI optimizations, and unified inbox. Generation without an outer-loop system just moves the queue.

Note the branding sunset: Diamond name deprecated Oct 2025 in favor of Graphite Agent—same capability class.

## Why it matters for code review

Useful vendor primary source for how stacking + AI review + merge automation are sold as one answer to agent-scale PR volume.
