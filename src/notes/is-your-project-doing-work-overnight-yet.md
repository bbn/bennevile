Peak-session mastery is real. You can still watch a coding agent crush a hard slice in a glory window and feel like the factory is winning. The better question is quieter: [is your project doing work overnight yet](https://www.anthropic.com/research/long-running-Claude)?

The itch behind that question is familiar to anyone living in agents. Leaving a lab or a repo idle used to be normal downtime. Now [every night without agents working for you is progress left on the table](/posts/anthropic-long-running-claude-scientific-computing/) — FOMO as opportunity cost. The productive answer is not a longer chat. It is infrastructure that keeps moving after you close the laptop: detachable runtimes, progress files, oracles, and stop conditions that refuse a fake “done.”

## Glory sessions vs compounding nights

A watched Claude Code session is still bounded by the human calendar. A regular week is roughly forty judgment hours; a grind stretches toward seventy-two; an agent’s week is a hundred sixty-eight clock hours — and agents do not take holidays. Those hours are not interchangeable (late grind ≠ morning judgment), which is exactly why the harness matters: it is how you spend the calendar without minting review debt.

The field is full of receipts that continuous motion compounds past heroics. Cursor’s self-driving harness ran [about a week with ~1,000 commits per hour](/posts/cursor-self-driving-codebases-week-harness/) once planners, workers, and handoffs replaced a single “keep going” session ([primary](https://cursor.com/blog/self-driving-codebases)). Stripe’s Minions pattern ships [1,300+ fully agent-produced PRs a week](/posts/stripe-minions-unattended-blueprint-harness/) by interleaving agent nodes with deterministic lint/push/CI and bounding repair loops ([primary](https://stripe.dev/blog/minions-stripes-one-shot-end-to-end-coding-agents-part-2)). Auto mode’s production unlock is continuity itself — [9× longer between interrupts](/posts/anthropic-auto-mode-overnight-production/), with Nuro’s 10pm–5am hill-climb waking to three PRs ([primary](https://claude.com/blog/auto-mode-in-production)). Smaller shops rhyme: [Ralph loops](/posts/crosley-ralph-loop-overnight-harness/) with stop hooks and machine-checkable done ([primary](https://blakecrosley.com/blog/ralph-agent-architecture)); [Nightwatch fleets](/posts/conveo-nightwatch-overnight-fleet/) that plan by day and ship overnight ([primary](https://engineering.conveo.ai/posts/nightwatch)); [headless Decode on Modal](/posts/decodingai-modal-headless-overnight-harness/) that turns cron into morning PR inventory ([primary](https://www.decodingai.com/p/coding-agents-in-remote-headless)); [Checkly’s harness-first overnight rewrite](/posts/checkly-overnight-harness-first-rewrite/); and the [overnight agent pattern](/posts/overnight-agent-pattern-codex/) written as queue → isolated exec → watchdog → morning triage ([primary](https://codex.danielvaughan.com/2026/08/04/overnight-agent-pattern-codex-cli-autonomous-scheduled-development-infrastructure-vps-cron-exec/)).

Picture a burn-down of remaining work. A harnessed line keeps falling through the grey overnight bands. A bursty mastery line drops hard in glory sessions, then flats — or ticks *up* as unverified debt arrives with the morning coffee.

## FOMO without a judge is just token theater

Overnight motion only redeems FOMO when delivery stops at verified inventory. Cognition’s own shift is the industry tell: [more agent sessions are now triggered asynchronously than by a human in the chair](/posts/technode-cognition-verification-bottleneck/), and generation stopped being scarce — verification is ([primary](https://technode.global/2026/09/15/enterprise-ai-code-verification-cognition-kevin-lee/)). [Long-running agents need harnesses, not hope](/posts/developersdigest-long-running-agents-need-harnesses/): task contracts, scoped workspaces, tool policy, checkpoints, verification gates, cost/time budgets, and a final receipt ([primary](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/long-running-agents-need-harnesses)). Cherny’s operating rule is the same invoice at Anthropic scale: [production Claude code needs a higher bar than human code](/posts/cherny-higher-bar-continuous-verification/), paid as continuous lint, tests, e2e, daily fuzzers, and automated review ([primary](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/11/boris-cherny/)).

Without that stack, overnight is expensive theater. One community overnight run fixed twelve design findings and passed an independent rebuild — then burned [158M tokens, ~95% re-reads](/posts/openai-community-overnight-158m-tokens-harness-tax/) ([primary](https://community.openai.com/t/two-agents-one-night-158-million-tokens-what-it-cost-and-what-we-are-fixing/1398068/1)). Verify-after still invoices the [Audit Tax](/posts/temrel-audit-tax-agent-verification/): ~90 seconds to draft a PR, ~90 minutes to trust it, with AI PRs taking 4.6× longer to review ([primary](https://dev.to/temrel/the-audit-tax-why-your-agent-made-you-slower-45bj)). [Verify-first harnesses](/posts/verify-first-harness-agent-pr-rejection-tax/) exist because **46%** of agent fix PRs never merge when gates sit outside the loop ([primary](https://www.theaioperator.net/p/coding-with-agents-the-verify-first)). And green CI is not a morning win condition: one autonomous Claude Code session [merged ~24 green PRs and left ≥9 live regressions](/posts/claude-code-24-green-prs-nine-regressions/) the same evening ([primary](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/86278)). Cursor’s week-long harness even found that [100% correctness before every commit](/posts/cursor-self-driving-codebases-week-harness/) serialized the factory to death — modest accepted slack plus periodic green reconciliation beat perfectionist heroics.

Dual-LLM [Night Shift](/posts/night-shift-dual-llm-overnight-harness/) patterns, independent rebuild judges, and blueprint-bounded CI are the same idea: overnight throughput compounds only when an unskippable judge exists *before* the human is the regression detector.

## Why it matters for code review

Review relocates. The scarce morning job is not replaying the chat; it is picking up a verified inventory — PRs with oracles, traces, and gates that already failed closed — and owning the harness that keeps that inventory honest across the night. Peak Claude mastery still wins afternoon slices. The projects that pull ahead are the ones whose remaining-work line keeps falling while everyone else is asleep.
