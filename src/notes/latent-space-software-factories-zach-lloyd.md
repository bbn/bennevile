Richard MacManus interviews Warp CEO Zach Lloyd at AI Engineer World’s Fair: factories as the shift from interactive agent use to automated triage→spec→implement→review→verify→ship→monitor loops. Oz walks orgs through choosing repos, lifecycle stations, and human-in-the-loop points.

Money quote for builders: most of the factory is not a new interface—it’s integration into Jira/Linear/Slack/GitHub. Factory engineering is meta-engineering: why an agent fails a task, what feedback/context/workflow change fixes it. Adoption path: start on lower-risk repos, raise auto-merge percentage deliberately.

Advice to practitioners: automate an annoying loop and discover whether review visibility is the real blocker.

## Why it matters for code review

Treats review as a configurable factory station with explicit human gates for high-risk changes—and as a loop you instrument, not a chat habit you hope scales.
