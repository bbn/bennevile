GitHub reports CCR growing to a meaningful share of reviews on the platform and narrates the shift from early thoroughness goals to high-signal, actionable comments—powered by memory, models, and tool calling.

## Why it matters for code review

This is the incumbent’s learning curve in public. The same quality axes (accuracy, signal, speed) show up in every serious vendor post; GitHub’s scale makes their thumbs-up loops a useful benchmark for what “good enough” looks like in the wild.
