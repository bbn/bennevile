Codacy pairs an AI Risk Hub (governance/inventory angle) with an AI Reviewer that blends static analysis and LLM context—explicitly aiming to avoid “slop reviews.”

## Why it matters for code review

Hybrid engines are a sensible bet: deterministic rules for known bad patterns, LLMs for intent/logic gaps. Evaluate whether Codacy’s hybrid actually reduces noise versus bolted-on chat on top of old linters.
