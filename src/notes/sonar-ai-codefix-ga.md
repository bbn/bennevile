Sonar’s AI CodeFix GA is remediation-first: LLMs propose fixes for Sonar findings inside SonarQube and IDEs, not a greenfield PR comment bot.

## Why it matters for code review

Different shape than CodeRabbit-style review. If your bottleneck is backlog burn-down of known SAST/quality issues, CodeFix/Remediation Agent territory matters more than chatty PR summaries.
